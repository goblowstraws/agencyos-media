## Black

The best-seller for a reason. Black disappears against an outfit and reappears when light hits the engraving. Pair it with anything dark and it reads as intentional jewellery, not an accessory someone clocked. If you buy one finish and stop thinking about it, buy Black.

---

## Steel

Raw and minimal. Steel sits closer to fine jewellery territory — the kind of piece that works at a rooftop bar as well as a festival. Good choice if your usual metals run silver.

---

## Gold

Warm, confident, loud on purpose. Gold catches crowd lighting in a way the cooler finishes do not. Wear it on the necklace and let it do the talking.

---

## Rose Gold

Softer than Gold, warmer than Steel. Rose Gold lands well in natural light and holds its own at night. The finish that photographs best in golden hour.

---

## Rainbow

Iridescent and finish-obsessed in the most literal sense. Rainbow shifts colour with the angle of light — it is the one people point at. If you want the conversation starter, this is it.
