# GoBlow Finish Guide: Which Metal Pendant Is Right for You?

Five finishes. One decision. Here is how to pick yours.

---

## The Five Finishes

**Black**
The best-seller for a reason. Matte, low-profile, disappears against an outfit but hits differently when the light catches the GOBLOW engraving. Pairs with everything. Draws the most questions. Start here if you are undecided.

**Steel**
Clean, minimal, cold-toned. Sits closest to raw metal and suits a monochrome or all-silver kit. The one that reads "designed object" more than "jewellery."

**Gold**
Warm, deliberate, visible. This is not understated. Wear it when you want the pendant to be part of the look, not hiding underneath it.

**Rose Gold**
Softer than Gold but still a statement. Works well layered with other rose-toned pieces. The finish that photographs best in warm light.

**Rainbow**
The conversation starter. Iridescent, shifts with movement, no two viewing angles look the same. The one people point at across the room. If you came to be noticed, this is it.

---

## How to Choose

**Lead with your existing metals.** Silver jewellery? Steel. Gold stacks? Gold or Rose Gold. No other metals? Black reads neutral across all of it.

**Think about your most-worn setting.** Outdoor festival in full sun — Rainbow and Gold earn their keep. Late-night indoor venues with low lighting — Black and Steel hold their own without looking flat.

**Consider wear frequency.** Black hides micro-scratches over time better than polished finishes. If it lives around your neck five nights a week, that matters.
