# How Vigil Guard Works: Your Street's Alert Network, Explained

---

Vigil Guard is a Melbourne-born safety app. Free to start. No alarm bills. No security company. Just your neighbours, connected and paying attention.

Here is exactly how it works.

---

## The Free Tier: Panic Network

When you sign up, you get three tools at no cost. No card required.

**Panic Switch**

Hold the button. Vigil Guard sends your live GPS location to your four chosen contacts in a parallel call tree. At the same time, it wakes Vigil Guard members on your street. Everyone hears from you at once, not one after another. If you need emergency services, you call 000. Vigil Guard alerts your people. That is its job.

**Lookout**

See something that feels off? One tap flags it. A single flag does nothing on its own. When three separate members validate the same behaviour in an area, a quiet alert goes out to nearby members. No pile-on, no naming, no drama. Just a calm heads-up to the street.

**Guardian Walk**

Set an arrival time before you leave. If you do not check in on arrival, Vigil Guard alerts your circle with your last known location. Good for a walk home, a late shift, a solo night out.

**Crime Watch Feed**

A map of reported crime in your area, drawn from sourced data across Victoria. Useful context. Not a scare tactic.

---

## The Guardian Tier: $5/Month

Guardian is an optional supporter tier for people who want to back the mission and get a few solo extras: a watched walk feature, Steady, and an expanded supporter radius. The founder rate is $5/month, locked for life. It moves to $9/month when the founding cohort closes. That is the only paid product in the app right now.

## What Is Coming (Not Available Yet)

**Sentry** is an AI camera watch feature, currently in closed beta. It is coming. It is not purchasable today.

## Why It Works This Way

A panic button that calls one person and waits is slow. Vigil Guard calls four people at once and wakes your street at the same time. Speed matters, and so does knowing real people nearby are aware.

The Lookout tool is built to resist misuse. One report is just a report. Three validated reports from three separate people become an alert. That threshold exists on purpose.

Guardian Walk works in the background. Set your arrival time, walk, check in. If life gets in the way and you forget, your circle hears from the app, not silence.

None of this replaces calling 000. Vigil Guard is what happens between you and your people in the seconds before, during, and after you need help.
